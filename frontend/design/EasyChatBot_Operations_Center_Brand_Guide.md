# EasyChatBot Operations Center Brand Guide

## Visual identity

Primary:
- EasyChatBot logo
- Friendly professional AI companion

Mascot:
- YETI - The Friendly Explorer

Typography:
- Nunito: application interface
- Fredoka: character and emotional elements
- Caveat: storytelling moments

## Operations Center direction

The Operations Center should feel:
- trustworthy
- friendly
- controlled
- transparent

YETI acts as the governance companion explaining:
- provider status
- delivery controls
- audit events
- operational health

## Core screens

1. Governance Overview
2. Audit Timeline
3. Cybersecurity Delivery Control
4. Provider Management Control
5. Scheduler Status

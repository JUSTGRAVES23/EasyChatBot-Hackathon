Status: APPROVED

APPROVED PRINCIPLES
-------------------
1. Authentication assurance is separate from authentication.
2. Authentication assurance is separate from authorization.
3. Authentication assurance is separate from transaction authorization.
4. A0 < A1 < A2 < A3.
5. Assurance cannot be caller-forged.
6. Evidence must originate from validated Identity Adapter output.
7. Evidence is immutable after validation.
8. Evidence must be bound to principal and audience.
9. Freshness must use trusted server/application time.
10. Higher assurance does not imply authorization.
11. Business services must not implement their own MFA logic.
12. Raw credentials/secrets must not enter assurance context.
13. Assurance failures must be deterministic and auditable.

APPROVED FAILURE SEMANTICS
--------------------------

INVALID_AUTHENTICATION_EVIDENCE
INVALID_PRINCIPAL_BINDING
INVALID_AUDIENCE

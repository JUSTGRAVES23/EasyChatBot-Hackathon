# EasyChatBot Beta 1 Privacy / GRC Operational Layer

Status: DRAFT — PENDING DPO, GRC and Legal review.

This package is the governance/evidence boundary around the Beta 1 Telegram
processing already implemented in the application.

It does not execute personal-data processing and must not be treated as a
substitute for legal advice, the formal ROPA, privacy notice, DPIA, contracts
or DPO approval.

## Scope

Only Beta 1 processing is represented:

- Telegram Beta access/authentication
- Telegram conversation processing
- pseudonymous conversation-event persistence
- privacy/quality validation before persistence
- provider/transfer assessment where an external AI provider is used

Future application conversations and future memory capabilities are not
treated as active Beta 1 processing unless separately approved.

## Sign-off rule

All records are DRAFT until:

1. Legal basis is confirmed for each purpose.
2. Retention periods/triggers are approved.
3. Processor/controller roles are confirmed.
4. International transfer analysis is completed where relevant.
5. DPIA screening is completed and any required DPIA is approved.
6. Transparency/privacy notice requirements are satisfied.
7. DPO and Head GRC sign off.

## Legal references

Primary references are the GDPR Articles listed in the workbook's
Legal_Article_Register. EUR-Lex is the authoritative source used for the
draft legal references.

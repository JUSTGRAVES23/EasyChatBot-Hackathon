# W15.6.1 Authentication Assurance Contract Hardening

## 1. Decision
- External/provider evidence is untrusted until validated by the Identity Adapter.
- Assurance fields are derived from validated evidence and policy; callers cannot self-assert A3.
- Freshness uses trusted server/application time.
- Assurance ordering is **A3 > A2 > A1 > A0**.
- Higher authentication assurance never implies authorization.
- Internal policy evaluation has explicit ALLOW/DENY semantics.
- Validated evidence is immutable after validation.
- Provider issuer, audience, subject, signature, expiry and related protocol checks are mandatory before evidence mapping.

## 2. Trust boundary
```text
External IAM/session evidence
        |
        v
Identity Adapter
  validate issuer/audience/signature/expiry/subject
  normalize authentication evidence
        |
        v
VALIDATED AUTHENTICATION EVIDENCE
        |
        v
Authentication Assurance Service
  trusted server time + policy
        |
        v
Assurance Context + Policy Decision
        |
     +--+--+
   ALLOW  DENY
```

## 3. Anti-forgery
A caller MUST NOT be able to submit assurance_level=A3, phishing_resistant=true, or user_verified=true as self-asserted proof. Only Identity Adapter-produced validated evidence may enter the assurance evaluator.

## 4. Evidence integrity
Validated evidence and resulting assurance context are immutable. Evidence retains issuer, subject, validated authentication time, method/strength, user-verification, phishing-resistance, correlation ID and policy version. Raw credentials, access/refresh tokens, OTP secrets and WebAuthn private material MUST NOT appear in assurance contexts, business payloads or logs.

## 5. Trusted time
Freshness MUST use a trusted server/application clock:
`trusted_now - validated_assurance_time <= policy freshness window`.
Caller-supplied current time MUST NOT influence freshness. Freshness windows are centrally policy-controlled.

## 6. Assurance ordering
`A3 > A2 > A1 > A0`.
This is an authentication-strength ordering only. It is NOT an authorization hierarchy. An A3 principal may still receive AUTHORIZATION_DENIED.

## 7. Structured policy decision
Internal result:
- ALLOW
- DENY

External deterministic failures remain distinct:
- UNAUTHENTICATED
- INVALID_AUTHENTICATION_EVIDENCE
- INVALID_PRINCIPAL_BINDING
- STALE_AUTHENTICATION_ASSURANCE
- INSUFFICIENT_AUTHENTICATION_ASSURANCE
- AUTHORIZATION_DENIED
- TRANSACTION_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED
- INVALID_TRANSACTION_AUTHORIZATION
- EXPIRED_TRANSACTION_AUTHORIZATION
- REPLAYED_TRANSACTION_AUTHORIZATION

## 8. No silent downgrade
If A2/A3 is required and evidence is missing, stale, weaker, invalid or untrusted, deny. Never silently downgrade.

## 9. Responsibility split
IAM may provide identity authentication, MFA and passkeys/WebAuthn. The Identity Adapter validates/normalizes provider evidence. EasyChatBot owns assurance policy, application authorization, transaction authorization, risk policy and audit/evidence correlation.

## 10. Transaction authorization remains separate
Authentication assurance asks how strongly/recently the principal authenticated. Transaction authorization asks whether the principal approved the exact transaction. A3 authentication does not authorize a payment, purchase, refund or subscription change.

## 11. Freeze condition
W15.6.1 is hardened when the four blockers are addressed, tests 17–21 are defined, production authentication code remains untouched, and W15.6.2 confirms the contract.

W15.8.1 — TRANSACTION AUTHORIZATION INTEGRATION ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATION

STATUS
W15.7: COMPLETE / VERIFIED
W15.7 commit: 03283d8
Security regression: 72 passed
W15.8.0 pre-build inspection: PASS

ARCHITECTURE
Authenticated Principal
    -> AuthorizationService
    -> Authentication Assurance
    -> Transaction Authorization
    -> Business Service
    -> Execute Operation

RULES
1. AuthorizationService remains authoritative for business permissions.
2. Transaction authorization does not replace business authorization.
3. High-risk operations require both authorization and transaction authorization.
4. Business services must not implement MFA.
5. Business services must not implement authentication freshness policy.
6. Business services must not integrate directly with external identity providers.
7. Transaction authorization must bind to the exact canonical transaction.
8. Missing transaction authorization fails closed.
9. Principal mismatch fails closed.
10. Security-relevant transaction changes invalidate approval.
11. Transaction authorization is single-use.
12. No payment or money movement implementation in W15.8.
13. Monetary thresholds remain centrally governed.
14. Existing unrelated worktree changes must remain untouched.

IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY
First identify concrete high-risk business-service gates.
Do not modify business services until the integration point is independently reviewed.
Do not bypass AuthorizationService.
Do not duplicate W15.6 assurance logic.
Do not duplicate W15.7 transaction authorization logic.

REQUIRED NEXT REVIEW
Independent 4-eyes review of the integration boundary before implementation.

END W15.8.1
